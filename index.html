Moontide

Live★★★★★5.0 · Arcade game · iPhone & iPad

Hold to pull the ocean toward the Moon, then let go and it crashes back down. It's a one-touch game where you steer the water, not the fish.

  • Prototyped on the web, then ported to 100% native Swift with zero dependencies. Golden-trace tests replay 1,100 recorded frames from the original and match the port to within 1e-11 px, so it feels exactly the same.
  • Custom Metal water shader (refraction, caustics, moonlight specular, film grain) on a 120 Hz CADisplayLink loop, plus a CoreHaptics rumble that follows the water's velocity.
  • Modular architecture: separate Core, Rendering, Audio and Platform Swift packages. The game core emits side effects as events, so 130+ tests check behavior without any UI.
  • Shipped as a complete product: Game Center, iCloud save sync, StoreKit 2 purchases and privacy-first, non-personalized ads. Four updates went out in the first two weeks.

SnapNotes

Live1,000+ downloads · Music · iPhone & iPad

Take a photo of any sheet music and it turns into falling notes you can play along to on a real piano.

  • Optical music recognition backend I built and run: FastAPI + Audiveris + Tesseract in Docker on Fly.io, with token auth, scale-to-zero and result caching.
  • Flutter front end with BLoC state management, a custom-painted 60 fps note visualizer, and MIDI keyboard input over USB and Bluetooth.
  • Native Swift integrations for document scanning, home-screen widgets and iCloud backup.
  • A real business: free tier plus a Pro subscription through StoreKit, a 254-song catalog, and about 370 automated tests kept passing from TestFlight through every release.

Hive Sort

In the worksPuzzle · iPhone & iPad

A hex-stack sorting puzzle. Drop stacks onto the hive, matching colors slide together, and ten of a color turn into honey.

  • Balanced by bots, not by guessing. I wrote simulators that play every Adventure level, measure win rates and propose fixes for levels that are off target. Daily Run seeds are checked for fairness the same way.
  • Automated QA: headless-Chrome playtests with real touch drags, rule checks over long runs, and a release checker that lists every remaining App Store blocker.
  • Zero image assets. Every tile, bee and icon is drawn in code. Classic, Adventure (50 hand-made levels plus endless ones) and Daily Run modes.
